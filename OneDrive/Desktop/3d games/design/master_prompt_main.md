## 2. Art style (one style everywhere)

- **Painted, saturated, matte** (the look of the 13 class models, which are in the clean anime game-art style of the **Time Mage** picture): clean shapes, soft colour ramps, no photo grit, **no mirror shine**. Only water, ice, gold and real metal stay glossy.
- Photo-scanned CC0 props go through the project's **style filter** (`maps/stylize_lib.py`: blur fine detail, richer colour, banded lightness, weak normal maps, flat matte metal/roughness). Hunyuan props only get a light matte touch.
- Ground, stone and walls are **procedural painted textures with normal maps** (`maps/surfaces.py`); water is rippled and see-through, ice is cracked and glossy, quicksand is a whirlpool, waterfalls are streaked with foam, toxic water glows green.
- **Walls come from Hunyuan wall models matching each map's style** (done for Desert Tomb, Pirate Cove, Shadow Crypt, Toxic Swamp, Clockwork Mines, Moon Sanctuary).
- **Lights are real lights**: lantern posts, crystals and boilers carry real point lights (exported in the map file), not just glowing textures.
- **Water is not a perfect circle**: irregular, asymmetrical outlines. All decals (curses, jump pads, lantern glow) look hand-painted, not geometric circles.
- **Paths slowly fade out** [YOURS]: dirt/ice/seabed paths have soft edges and fade out gradually at their ends (vertex-alpha ribbons), never a hard cut.
- **Map borders belong to the map** [YOURS]: never a bare cliff or wall. Each map's edge is built from that map's own theme (woodland that thickens, coral and kelp, basalt fields, snow pines and icebergs, sandstone canyon, graveyard dead woods behind a cemetery wall, crystal and stalagmites, mangrove forest, ore rock face with tunnel mouths, marble pillars and floating stones, giant chess pieces). The Flora Forest barrier "looked out of place" when it was a hedge of identical trees: edges must look like a natural place, thin at the playable edge and thicker, taller and lower-detail (LOD) farther out.
